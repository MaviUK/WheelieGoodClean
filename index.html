FAQ

Good questions. Clean answers.

When should I leave my bin out?

Please leave it accessible after your normal council collection so we can clean it once it has been emptied.

Can you clean more than one bin?

Yes. Add as many bins as you need in the booking form and we will confirm the quote with you.

Do you offer one-off cleans?

Yes. Choose one-off in the booking form, or select regular cleaning if you want to stay on a route.

Do you clean commercial bins?

Yes. Tell us the bin size and location and we will confirm availability and pricing.

Booking terms
Bins must be empty and accessible on the agreed cleaning day. If access is blocked or the bin has not been emptied, the clean may need to be rearranged. Some staining may not fully remove in one visit. We will confirm service availability and any price before the booking is final.
